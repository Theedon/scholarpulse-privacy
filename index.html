ScholarPulse Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

ScholarPulse is a free Android app for browsing, searching, reading and saving AI research papers from arXiv. It is published by Theedon, an independent developer. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

  • The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.
  • We don't run a server for the app, and we don't receive your searches or reading list.
  • Your saved papers stay on your device.
  • The app connects directly to arXiv to get papers.

Information sent to arXiv

To show papers, the app connects directly from your device to arXiv. When you browse the feed, search, or open a paper, arXiv receives that request, including any search terms you enter and the paper you ask for. Like any website, arXiv also receives technical details such as your IP address. These connections are encrypted in transit.

arXiv is an independent service, and its own privacy policy applies to the information it receives. We don't control how arXiv handles that information.

Information stored on your device

Papers you save are stored only on your device, so they stay available in your reading list. This information is not sent to us. You can remove a saved paper at any time in the app, and uninstalling the app deletes your saved list.

Data retention and deletion

We don't run a server for the app, so we don't hold or retain any of your information. Your saved papers stay on your device until you remove them in the app or uninstall the app, which deletes them. Because we never receive your searches or paper requests, there is nothing for us to delete. For information arXiv receives, see arXiv's privacy policy.

Security

The app connects to arXiv over HTTPS, so your requests are encrypted in transit. Saved papers are kept in the app's private storage on your device.

Information we don't collect

We don't ask for your name, email address or any other account details. We don't use advertising or analytics services, and we don't sell or share your information.

Links to other services

The app can open arXiv pages and PDFs in your browser or another app. Those services have their own privacy practices.

Children

ScholarPulse is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
